CapturePro

Privacy Policy

CapturePro is a Chrome extension for recording, editing and exporting screen recordings. It works entirely on your device: it has no servers, no accounts and no tracking.

Effective November 3, 2026 · Applies to CapturePro 1.0 and later
✓Nothing is collectedNo personal data is gathered, sent or sold.
✓Stays on your deviceRecordings are saved only in your browser.
✓You stay in controlDevices turn on only when you ask, through Chrome's prompts.

What CapturePro accesses

  • Your screen, a window or a tab, only after you click Start recording and pick a source in Chrome's own picker.
  • Microphone and camera, only if you turn them on, after Chrome asks for your permission.
  • System or tab audio, only if you tick “Share audio” in Chrome's picker.

What CapturePro stores

  • Recordings, thumbnails and edits are saved in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your device.
  • Preferences such as language, theme, color palette, recording quality and chosen devices are saved in Chrome's extension storage on your device.

You can delete any recording from the library at any time. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionUsed for
storageRemembering your preferences and showing the current recording status in the toolbar.
unlimitedStorageSaving long or 4K recordings on your device without hitting the browser's default size limit.
Screen, microphone, cameraRecording, only after you start a recording or turn a device on and Chrome asks you.

CapturePro does not request access to the websites you visit and does not read their content.

What CapturePro does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.
  • It has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not upload your recordings. Files leave your device only when you choose to download them.
  • It does not load code from the internet. Everything it runs is included in the extension.

Children

CapturePro does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
